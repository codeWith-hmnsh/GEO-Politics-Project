# Statecraft 4.0 — Engagement and Retention

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 4.0 (final, 2026-10-11) |
| Date | 2026-10-11 |
| Related | [GAME-DESIGN](GAME-DESIGN.md) · [UI-DESIGN](UI-DESIGN.md) |

Why a player enjoys a turn, finishes a match, and comes back tomorrow. Every loop here must be honest: no fake urgency, no paid power, no punishment for missing a day.

---

## 1. Inside a turn (seconds)

| Moment | Feeling | How |
|---|---|---|
| Briefing | "Something is happening and it is my problem" | A crisis that names your country; advisors who disagree |
| Moving a slider | "My decision has weight" | The forecast changes as you drag |
| Playing a card | Control | Card flies to the orders tray; PC dots drain |
| A proposal answered | "Someone is reacting to me" | Card flip, knock sound, Counter highlights |
| Lock | Commitment | Stamp and sound |

## 2. The reveal

The reveal is the biggest moment of every turn ([UI-DESIGN §5.7](UI-DESIGN.md#57-the-reveal-resolution-replay)):

1. **Six moves flip together.** You see what everyone did at the same instant.
2. **The world moves:** flows, sanctions and events draw on the globe.
3. **Your numbers count**, one after another.
4. **Your colour spreads.** Partner regions you lead re-tint on the globe — progress you can see on the map.
5. **The ranking reorders.** Climbing a place is the strongest repeated reward in the game.
6. **Forecast vs actual:** "Forecast: energy ▲9. Actual: ▲5 — US sanctions delayed 1 unit." The gap turns other leaders into the story.

## 3. Across a match (minutes)

- **A clear ambition:** your Power rank is always visible. Rank 1 is the dream; your mission is the plan.
- **Threads:** a deal that runs for 3–5 turns is a small story with an ending. A broken promise is a twist.
- **Payoffs that arrive later:** a university started in 2027 lifts skills in 2030. Patience is rewarded on screen.
- **Comebacks:** Leader Score measures improvement, so the smallest country can still win.
- **Mid-match objectives** (two per scenario) give short goals between the big ones.
- **World Votes** on turns 3, 6 and 9: the one moment every leader acts on the same question, and the votes flip together.
- **Early warnings** turn a disaster into a decision: the player sees trouble one turn ahead and can act.
- **Headlines:** players want their move to be the headline of the year.

## 4. After a match (the reason to play again)

- **Your World History and share card:** a story the player made, worth showing to friends.
- **Rematch:** same players, new seed — "this time I will not trust Russia".
- **Different scenario next time:** Energy Shock, Food Crisis, Trade War and Calm Start play very differently.
- **Different country next time:** each country has its own problem (India: jobs; Japan: energy; Saudi Arabia: life after oil).
- **"What if?" (S7):** pick one past decision, change it, and watch a quick re-simulation of the rest against bots. Unranked and labelled.

## 5. Progression (S7)

| Element | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Leader level** | XP from finishing matches (more for winning, objectives and missions). Cosmetic only. |
| **Titles** | Earned by play style across matches: *The Economist* (best growth), *The Dealmaker* (most treaties kept), *The Hawk* (highest security), *The Builder* (most projects), *Steady Hand* (highest stability). Shown on the share card. |
| **Country mastery** | A small badge per country: win once, win on Hard, complete the mission. |
| **Scenario unlocks** | New players start with Energy Shock and Calm Start. Food Crisis and Trade War unlock after the first two matches. This keeps the first matches simple. |
| **Achievements** | Tied to real lessons: "Survive an oil shock without debt above 100%", "Win with Japan", "End a sanction you started". |

Nothing in progression gives power in a match. Every seat starts equal in the rules.

## 6. Across days (S7–S8)

- **Daily Crisis:** one shared solo crisis per day, 3 decisions, about 2 minutes, the same for everyone. A score and a spoiler-free share grid (Wordle style). No streak punishment.
- **Weekly world:** one shared seed per week with a public leaderboard, so friends compare the same starting world.
- **Friends' rooms:** Create room and Join with code stay the strongest reason to return.
- **Async season (S8):** 1 turn per day for 12 days; a 2-minute visit each day.

## 7. Learning as a reward

- The Why chain makes the player feel smart: "I understood why that happened."
- Real World notes connect a move to history in one line.
- Term chips explain a concept the moment it matters.
- After a match, a "You met these ideas" list shows 3–5 concepts with links to the 1.0 glossary.

## 8. Guard rails

- No loot boxes, no countdown sales, no streak punishment, no energy systems that block play.
- A match always ends in about 25 minutes, and the game says so before it starts.
- Monetization follows D-1: depth, comfort and style only. Never power in ranked.
- Simulated content is always labelled **Simulated**. Real countries are treated neutrally, as in the 1.0 content guide.

## 9. Metrics

| Metric | Target for the S3 solo playtest | Target after S6 |
|---|---|---|
| Players who finish their first match | > 70% | > 75% |
| Players who start a second match the same day | > 40% | > 45% |
| Players who open a Why chain at least once per match | > 60% | > 60% |
| Players who send at least one proposal per match | > 50% | > 65% |
| Day-7 return | — | > 20% |
| Share card shared or downloaded | — | > 10% of finished matches |
| Players who can say in their own words why one of their numbers moved | 4 of 5 testers | — |
