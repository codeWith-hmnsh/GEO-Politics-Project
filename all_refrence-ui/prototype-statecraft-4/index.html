How a year works

One turn is one year.

1Briefing

The world hands you a problem.

A tanker is attacked in the Strait of Hormuz. Oil is up 18%. Your advisors disagree, and you have about 90 seconds.

2Budget

Decide where the money goes.

Revenue comes from your GDP. Move money between seven ministries. Education pays off in three years; welfare calms people now. Last year's budget carries over.

3Build

Build what your country lacks.

Refineries, ports, chip fabs, universities. Each project takes one to four years and unlocks a real capability. No workers, no grind.

4Act and negotiate

Make your moves. Make your deals.

Play the five or six cards that matter this year. Offer a deal: what you give, what you want, for how long. Leaders accept, refuse or counter.

5Lock · the world answers

Everyone moves at once.

All six leaders lock. Their moves collide. Oil flows, sanctions bite, your colour spreads across the regions you support, and the ranking moves.

Learn by leading

You will meet real ideas.

Trade

Sanctions leakage

Sanctions rarely stop trade fully. Goods find other buyers at a discount, through third countries.

In the game: half the blocked oil still reaches the market when one big economy sanctions alone.
Jobs

Okun's law

A growing workforce needs growth just to keep unemployment flat. Faster growth cuts unemployment; slower growth raises it.

In the game: India needs about 6.5% growth before unemployment starts to fall.
Markets

Terms of trade

When oil gets expensive, exporters gain and importers pay. Long contracts protect a buyer from price spikes.

In the game: a treaty fixes the price at signing for its whole term.
Questions

Before you take office

How long is a match?

Twelve turns of about 90 seconds, plus a short replay after each. Around 22 minutes. When every leader locks early, the turn ends early.

Do I need friends online?

No. Start puts you in a world within 40 seconds. Bots lead every country that has no human, and a bot takes over if a player leaves.

Is there a war?

Not in the first version. Military strength works through deterrence and security. Economy, trade and diplomacy decide the match.

How is the winner chosen?

By Leader Score: how much you improved your country against a "do nothing" world, plus your mission, your rank climb and the scenario objectives.

Is it free?

Yes. No download, no login to play. Nothing you can buy gives power in ranked play.
