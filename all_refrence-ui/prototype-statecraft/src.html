2How it works

Four steps, about 30 minutes

New players read this once. It explains the whole loop before they commit to a match.

1

Press Start

No modes to choose. Start takes you straight into the world that is running now.

3Pick your country

Who will you lead?

Everyone who presses Start lands here and joins the same world. Free countries are open; taken ones show their leader. Picking a card flies the globe in section 1 to that country.

  • 3Card shows starting strength, weakness and difficulty, so a new player can choose quickly.
  • 3Free country is run by a bot until you take it. You take over from the current round.
  • 3World full (10 players): the next player who presses Start opens a new world.

4Inside the world

The match screen

In the real game this opens full-screen. Left is you, right is the country you picked on the globe, bottom is what you decide this round.

  • ATop bar: round, quarter, timer, world tension.
  • BCabinet: your money, approval, economy, goods.
  • CGlobe: status colours, trade and sanction lines, players. Tap a country.
  • DTarget panel: that country, and the actions you can take.
  • ECategory dock: Domestic, Economy, Trade, Diplomacy, Military.
  • FOrders: up to 3 decisions, then Submit.
  • GInbox and World Wire: requests and simulated news.

Try it Pick a category E, tap an action in the right panel D, it drops into an order slot F, then press Submit. Tap other countries on the globe C.
